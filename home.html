The two communities

One expressway. Two very different investments.

Gulberg Islamabad is really two projects wearing one name. GDIP treats them separately because buyers, plot sizes and timelines are not the same.

Gulberg Greens

Farmhouse living, low density

Six primary blocks — A-Executive, A, B, C, D and E — spread across large-format plots built for privacy and long-term appreciation rather than quick turnover.

  • Plot sizes from 4 to 10 Kanal
  • Block D reserved for commercial use
  • Best suited to long-horizon, high-value holding
Gulberg Residencia

Family housing, active resale

Dozens of blocks from AA through V, including multiple Executive enclaves, offer a much wider entry point and a livelier resale market near D-Markaz.

  • Plot sizes from 5 Marla to 2 Kanal
  • Blocks A–D and E–I are the most developed
  • Blocks Q–T are newer, early-stage launches
